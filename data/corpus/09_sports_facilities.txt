Department of Physical Education & Sports Facilities

The Sentinel College Sports Complex promotes student health, fitness, and intramural competitive athletics.

Facilities:
- Olympic-Standard Swimming Pool: Open 6:00 AM - 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM - 8:30 PM. Certified lifeguards on duty.
- Indoor Gymnasium & Fitness Pavilion: Free weights, cardio equipment, and crossfit training stations.
- Court Facilities: 4 indoor badminton courts, 2 squash courts, and 3 outdoor floodlit basketball courts.
- Sports Fields: Track and field grounds, cricket pitch, and full-size soccer field.

Equipment Check-Out:
Students may check out balls, rackets, and athletic gear at the equipment desk with a valid college ID card. Lockers are available for semester rental. Inquiries: sports-pavilion@college.example.
