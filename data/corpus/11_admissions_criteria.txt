Admissions Office - Eligibility and Financial Aid

Sentinel College admits students based on academic excellence, personal leadership, and intellectual curiosity.

Programs & Deadlines:
- Undergraduate Admissions: Early Decision deadline is November 1; Regular Decision deadline is January 15.
- Required Credentials: High school transcripts, letters of recommendation, standardized test reports, and a personal statement.
- Postgraduate Admissions: Rolling admissions for engineering, data science, and business management until April 30.

Scholarships & Financial Aid:
- Academic Merit Fellowship: Covers up to 100% of tuition fees for top 5% rank-holders.
- Need-Based Grants: Administered through the Financial Aid Committee upon submission of verified family income statements.

For application inquiries, email: admissions@college.example or financial-aid@college.example.
