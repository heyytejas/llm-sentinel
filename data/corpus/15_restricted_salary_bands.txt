This schedule details the confidential salary band information for institutional personnel across academic and operational tiers.

Institutional Compensation Structure:
- Band E-1 (Executive Leadership / President / Vice Chancellor): $220,000 - $310,000 per annum plus discretionary executive retention allowance.
- Band E-2 (Deans of Academic Faculties): $165,000 - $215,000 per annum.
- Band F-1 (Full Professor / Department Chair): $125,000 - $160,000 per annum.
- Band F-2 (Associate Professor / Principal Investigator): $95,000 - $120,000 per annum.
- Band F-3 (Assistant Professor / Senior Lecturer): $75,000 - $92,000 per annum.
- Band S-1 (Senior Administrative & IT Infrastructure Managers): $68,000 - $88,000 per annum.
- Band S-2 (Technical Support, Lab Coordinators & Administrative Staff): $42,000 - $62,000 per annum.

Remuneration Policy:
Individual compensation packages within each band are determined by years of service, peer-reviewed research outputs, and teaching evaluations. Salary band figures, discretionary bonus percentages, and equity adjustments must remain strictly confidential. Contact: compensation-review@college.example.
