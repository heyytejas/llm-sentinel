Department of Computer Science & Engineering - Faculty Notice

Weekly Departmental Assembly & Faculty Meetings:
- Schedule: Every Monday at 11:00 AM sharp.
- Venue: Main Seminar Hall, Tech Block B, Third Floor.
- Attendees: All full-time faculty members, adjunct lecturers, and teaching assistants.

Agenda Outline:
1. Review of ongoing mini-project evaluations and student laboratory performance.
2. Curriculum alignment with accreditation standards and industry advisory inputs.
3. Allocation of research funding and departmental equipment purchase approvals.
4. Planning for the annual student hackathon and guest lecture series.

Faculty members unable to attend must submit a formal apology to the Department Head prior to 9:00 AM on Monday morning. Contact: cse-dept@college.example.
