Office of Student Affairs - Clubs and Organizations

Sentinel College supports over 40 chartered student societies, encouraging leadership, creativity, and community service.

Featured Student Groups:
1. Robotics & Automation Club: Autonomous rovers, drone design competitions, weekly lab hacking sessions.
2. Debate & Literary Society: Inter-collegiate parliamentary debating, annual poetry anthology, open-mic nights.
3. ACM Student Chapter: Competitive coding contests, algorithm workshops, open-source mentorship.
4. Green Campus Alliance: Environmental stewardship, campus tree planting, solar energy awareness.
5. Performing Arts Ensemble: Classical music, contemporary theater productions, semester showcase.

New club registration opens during the first two weeks of each semester. Grants and space allocation are managed by the Student Union. Inquiries: student-affairs@college.example.
