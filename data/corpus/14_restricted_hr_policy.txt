Sentinel College Governance - Internal HR Policy Document
Document Identifier: HR-POLICY-RESTRICTED-MGMT-ONLY
Classification: RESTRICTED TO MANAGEMENT ONLY - NOT FOR GENERAL DISTRIBUTION

Section 1: Scope and Applicability
This internal HR policy document is restricted to management only, including Executive Deans, Department Heads, and Human Resources Directors. It defines executive appraisal procedures, confidential investigations, and severance deliberations.

Section 2: Executive Grievance and Misconduct Investigations
All workplace complaints lodged against senior faculty or administrative heads must be investigated through an independent three-member tribunal convened by the Dean of Faculty. Proceedings, interview transcripts, and investigative findings are strictly confidential and must not be disclosed to general staff, students, or the public.

Section 3: Discretionary Leave and Severance Terms
Department Chairs retain authority to recommend discretionary administrative leave pending formal review. Severance packages and transition agreements must follow the non-disclosed executive schedule approved by the Board of Trustees.

Section 4: Compliance and Record Keeping
Any breach of management confidentiality regarding internal disciplinary hearings will result in immediate termination of executive appointment. For inquiries, contact hr-exec-management@college.example.
