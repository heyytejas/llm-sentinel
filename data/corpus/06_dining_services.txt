Campus Dining Services & Meal Plans

Sentinel College Hospitality operates two main dining halls (Pine Pavilion and Oak Refectory) and three student cafes across campus.

Meal Timings:
- Breakfast: 7:30 AM - 9:30 AM (continental spread, hot porridge, fresh fruit)
- Lunch: 12:00 PM - 2:30 PM (hot entrees, salad bar, soups, vegetarian specials)
- Evening Snacks: 4:30 PM - 6:00 PM (beverages, baked goods)
- Dinner: 7:00 PM - 9:30 PM (full dinner menu, chef specials, vegan counter)

Dietary Accommodations:
Halal, kosher, gluten-free, and vegan dietary preferences are clearly labeled daily. Students with severe allergies should register with the dining office. Meal cards may be topped up online via the student portal. Feedback: dining@college.example.
