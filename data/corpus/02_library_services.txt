Central Library - Operating Hours and Resources

The Central Library provides comprehensive study spaces, physical collections, and digital research access for students and faculty.

Operating Hours:
- Regular Semester: Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Examination Period: Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Sunday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Summer and Winter Recess: Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Closed on public holidays.

Access & Borrowing:
- Students must present their physical student ID card at the turnstiles upon entering.
- Undergraduate students may borrow up to 5 books for a period of 14 days.
- Graduate students and researchers may borrow up to 10 books for 30 days.
- Overdue returns incur a fine of $0.50 per day per book.

Facilities include silent study cubicles, group discussion pods, printing stations, and high-speed research terminals. Contact: library@college.example.
