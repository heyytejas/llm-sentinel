Central Examination Cell - Semester Examination Schedule

The Central Examination Cell coordinates all undergraduate and postgraduate final assessments at Sentinel College.

Key Regulations:
1. The regular semester examinations commence on the second Monday of November each year and run for three weeks.
2. Examination sessions are split into Morning (9:30 AM - 12:30 PM) and Afternoon (2:00 PM - 5:00 PM).
3. Timetables are published on the campus intranet four weeks prior to commencement.
4. Students experiencing scheduling conflicts or paper clashes must notify the Examination Cell in writing by Friday before exams begin.
5. Hall tickets and valid student identity cards are mandatory for entry into examination halls.
6. Electronic devices, smartwatches, and programmable calculators are strictly prohibited inside the hall.

For questions, contact: exam-cell@college.example or visit Room 204, Administrative Block.
