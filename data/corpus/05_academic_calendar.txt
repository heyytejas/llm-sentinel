Academic Affairs - Annual Academic Calendar (2026 - 2027)

Fall Semester:
- Orientation & Registration: August 18 - August 22
- Instruction Begins: August 25
- Mid-Semester Assessments: October 6 - October 10
- Semester Final Examinations: November 10 - November 28
- Winter Break: December 1 - January 4

Spring Semester:
- Registration & Commencement: January 5
- Spring Mid-Term Break: March 16 - March 20
- Final Assessments: April 27 - May 15
- Annual Convocation & Degree Awarding: June 5

All requests for late registration or academic leave must be formally endorsed by the respective faculty Dean. Inquiries: academic-affairs@college.example.
