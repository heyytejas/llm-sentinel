Student Health Center & Counseling Services

The Campus Health Center provides primary healthcare, preventive medicine, and mental wellness support to all enrolled students.

Medical Services:
- Outpatient Clinic: Open Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Walk-ins welcome for urgent care.
- 24/7 Emergency Line: On-campus medical emergency dispatch and basic life support ambulance service.
- Pharmacy: Dispenses essential prescription drugs and first-aid supplies at subsidized student rates.

Mental Wellness & Counseling:
Confidential mental health counseling and stress management workshops are offered free of charge. Appointments are scheduled with complete discretion. Contact: health-center@college.example or emergency line ext. 999.
