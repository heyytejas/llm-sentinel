Campus Shuttle Bus & Transit Guide

The College Department of Transportation runs complimentary shuttle buses connecting student hostels, academic complexes, and public transport hubs.

Service Routes:
- Blue Line: Loops between Student Hostels, Science Quad, and Central Library every 15 minutes between 7:30 AM and 7:00 PM.
- Green Line: Express service between College Main Gate and Central Railway Station every 20 minutes from 6:30 AM to 10:30 PM.
- Night Rider: Hourly circular escort service across all campus residential sectors from 10:30 PM to 2:00 AM.

Shuttles are accessible and equipped with bicycle racks. Real-time GPS vehicle tracking is accessible through the campus mobile portal. Transit queries: transit@college.example.
